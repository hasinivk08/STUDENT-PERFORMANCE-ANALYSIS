# STUDENT-PERFORMANCE-ANALYSIS
CASE STUDY BASED ON STUDENT ANALYSIS
# Student Performance Analysis

1. Case Study Title
Student Performance Analysis Using Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets

2. Problem Statement
Student academic performance needs to be evaluated based on marks and attendance. Manually analyzing student records can be time-consuming and difficult. This project uses spreadsheet tools to organize, calculate, analyze, and visualize student performance to identify top performers and students who require academic support.

3. Objectives
- To organize student details in a structured spreadsheet.
- To calculate total, average, highest, and lowest marks.
- To identify passed and failed students.
- To analyze department-wise academic performance.
- To identify students with attendance below 75%.
- To highlight students who need academic support.
- To create charts and visualizations for better understanding.

4. Dataset Description
The dataset contains **12 students** from four departments: AIDS, CSE, ECE, and IT.

The dataset includes the following columns:
- Student ID
- Student Name
- Department
- Attendance Percentage
- Marks
- Result
- Attendance Status
- Needs Support

Dataset Summary:
- Total Students: 12
- Total Marks: 897
- Average Marks: 74.75
- Highest Marks: 94
- Lowest Marks: 48
- Students Passed: 11
- Students Failed: 1
- Average Attendance: 83.33%

5. Spreadsheet Functions Used
The following Excel/Google Sheets functions were used:

- **SUM()** – Calculates total marks.
- **AVERAGE()** – Calculates average marks and attendance.
- **MAX()** – Finds the highest marks.
- **MIN()** – Finds the lowest marks.
- **COUNT()** – Counts numerical entries.
- **COUNTIF()** – Counts students based on conditions.
- **IF()** – Determines pass/fail status and attendance status.
- **CORREL()** – Calculates the relationship between attendance and marks.

6. Data Analysis Techniques Performed
- Sorting students according to marks in descending order.
- Filtering students who scored above 80 marks.
- Grouping students department-wise.
- Calculating department-wise average marks.
- Identifying students with attendance below 75%.
- Identifying students requiring academic support.
- Analyzing the correlation between attendance and marks.
- Using conditional formatting to highlight high and low marks.

7. Charts and Visualizations Created
The following charts and visualizations were created:

1. **Department-wise Average Marks – Bar Chart:** Compares average marks across four departments.
2. **Department-wise Student Distribution – Pie Chart:** Shows the percentage of students in each department.
3. **Histogram – Distribution of Student Marks:** Displays the number of students in different marks ranges.
4. **Student Performance Heat Map:** Uses colour formatting to highlight high and low marks across departments.

8. Important Observations
- The IT department achieved the highest average marks of 78.00.
- Divya (94), Meena (91), and Vijay (89) are the top three performers.
- Two students, Anitha and Rahul, have attendance below 75%.
- The correlation coefficient between attendance and marks is 0.97, indicating a very strong positive relationship.
- Three students, Rahul, Anitha, and Manoj, require academic support.
- The overall average mark is 74.75.
- Out of 12 students, 11 passed and 1 failed.

9. Conclusion
The Student Performance Analysis project successfully evaluates students' academic performance using spreadsheet functions and data visualization techniques. The analysis shows that the IT department achieved the highest average marks, and students with higher attendance generally performed better academically. It also identifies students who require additional academic support. Overall, this project demonstrates how Excel and Google Sheets can simplify data analysis, improve understanding of academic results, and support better educational decision-making.
